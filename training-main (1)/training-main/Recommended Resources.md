# Recommended Resources

There is a lot of machine learning material online, but some of it is junk (e.g. 80% of the posts on Medium). This is a list of resources that I find comprehensive, formal and clear.

# Courses

## Classic ML

- [Learning From Data](https://work.caltech.edu/telecourse.html) - Machine Learning online course taught by Caltech Professor Yaser Abu-Mostafa.
- [Foundations of Machine Learning](https://bloomberg.github.io/foml/#home) - This course covers a wide variety of topics in machine learning and statistical modeling. Instructor - David S. Rosenberg, Office of the CTO at Bloomberg.
- Technion Intro to ML Course

## Computer Vision

- [Stanford CS231n](http://cs231n.stanford.edu/) - Convolutional Neural Networks for Visual Recognition.

## Natural Language Processing

- [Stanford CS224n](http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/) - Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning.
- [BIU 89-680](https://u.cs.biu.ac.il/~89-680/) - Natural Language Processing (by Yoav Goldberg, Ido Dagan and Reut Tzarfaty).
- [BIU 89-687](https://u.cs.biu.ac.il/~89-687/) - Deep Learning for Text and Sequences (by Yoav Goldberg).

## Reinforcement Learning

- [Introduction to Reinforcement Learning](https://deepmind.com/learning-resources/-introduction-reinforcement-learning-david-silver) - with David Silver (DeepMind)
- [Berkeley CS285](http://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/) - Deep Reinforcement Learning (by Sergey Levine)

## Graph Neural Networks

- [Stanford CS224W](http://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224w/) - Machine Learning with Graphs.

## Other

- [Deep Generative Models](https://www.eng.biu.ac.il/fetayae/deepgen19/) by Ethan Fetaya
- [Stanford CS228](https://ermongroup.github.io/cs228-notes/) - Probabilistic Graphical Models
- [Categories for AI](https://cats.for.ai/program/) - by Petar Veličković and friends

# Books

- [Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning](http://users.isr.ist.utl.pt/~wurmd/Livros/school/Bishop%20-%20Pattern%20Recognition%20And%20Machine%20Learning%20-%20Springer%20%202006.pdf) - by Christopher M. Bishop. Free PDF Online.
